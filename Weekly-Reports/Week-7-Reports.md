# Week 7 Progress Report: Motion Optimization and Engraving Accuracy

## Overview

Week 7 was dedicated to optimizing the performance of the PCB prototyping machine by studying the relationship between motor speed, travel resolution, and laser power. Multiple machine configurations were evaluated to improve engraving quality while maintaining stable motion. Research was also conducted on integrating a display interface into the next version of the machine.

## Tasks Completed

- Investigated the effects of motor speed, travel resolution, and laser power on engraving performance.
- Performed multiple configuration and calibration tests to optimize motor speed.
- Successfully generated more accurate engraved images compared to previous iterations.
- Evaluated different machine parameters, including feed rate and travel resolution, to improve motion accuracy.

## Challenges Faced

- Machine speed remained significantly lower than the desired operating range when compared with similar CNC and 3D printer systems.
- Increasing the maximum feed rate caused the stepper motors to skip steps, resulting in positioning errors.
- Balancing engraving accuracy with higher machine speeds required further optimization and calibration.

## Key Outcomes

- Improved engraving accuracy through systematic parameter tuning.
- Gained a deeper understanding of the relationship between feed rate, travel resolution, and laser power.
- Identified the practical limits of the current motion control setup for future hardware and firmware improvements.

## Next Steps

- Further optimize motion parameters to increase machine speed without compromising accuracy.
- Achieve reliable and accurate image engraving for complex patterns.
- Begin integrating the display module into the CNC machine for improved user interaction and system monitoring.
