# Week 8 Progress Report: CNC Laser Engraving Calibration

## Overview

- Focused on calibrating the CNC laser engraving system for better accuracy and consistent engraving quality.
- Conducted multiple engraving tests to identify and troubleshoot double-line tracing.
- Tested different engraving speeds and calibration parameters.

## Tasks Completed

- Performed multiple CNC calibration and engraving tests.
- Engraved images and test patterns using different speed settings.
- Evaluated line accuracy, spacing, image quality, and motion consistency.
- Compared engraving results under different calibration conditions.

## Challenges Faced

- Double/duplicate engraving lines appeared consistently during testing.
- Changing engraving speeds did not significantly improve the issue.
- Further investigation is required into steps/mm, mechanical backlash, belt tension, microstepping, acceleration, laser timing, and G-code/toolpath generation.

## Key Outcomes

- Established a baseline for CNC engraving performance.
- Confirmed that the double-line issue is repeatable and requires further troubleshooting.
- Identified key parameters that need to be tested for further optimization.

## Next Steps

- Identify and eliminate the cause of double-line tracing.
- Verify X/Y steps-per-mm and mechanical alignment.
- Check belt tension, backlash, and microstepping.
- Inspect G-code/toolpaths for duplicate paths.
- Optimize engraving speed, laser power, and image resolution.
- Produce a clean and accurate reference engraving.
