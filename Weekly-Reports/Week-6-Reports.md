# Week 6 Progress Report: Machine Optimization and Prototype Fabrication

## Overview

-Week 6 focused on improving the performance of the PCB prototyping machine through motion parameter optimization and mechanical fabrication. Extensive testing was carried out to increase machine speed while maintaining engraving accuracy. The soldering iron PCB was successfully fabricated, and the base plate for the next-generation CNC prototype was manufactured using a metal cutting machine. Alongside hardware development, CAD skills were further enhanced through Fusion 360 modeling exercises.

## Tasks Completed

- Investigated and optimized machine speed and laser power parameters for improved CNC performance.
- Successfully fabricated the soldering iron PCB for hardware validation.
- Learned the safe operation of the metal cutting machine, including accurate straight and angled cuts.
- Created a robotic arm model in Fusion 360 to further develop CAD design skills.
- Integrated and tested updated speed parameters within the CNC control system.

## Challenges Faced

- Despite multiple configuration changes, the machine speed remained lower than the desired operating range.
- Increasing the motion speed affected dimensional accuracy, resulting in slightly enlarged engraved geometries.
- Maintaining an optimal balance between speed, positioning accuracy, and engraving quality required additional calibration and testing.

## Key Outcomes

- Successfully fabricated the base structure for the next-generation CNC machine.
- Improved understanding of machine parameter tuning and motion optimization.
- Enhanced practical manufacturing skills through metal cutting operations.
- Continued strengthening CAD design proficiency by developing a robotic arm model in Fusion 360.

## Next Steps

- Further optimize machine speed while preserving dimensional accuracy.
- Continue mechanical assembly of the new CNC machine prototype.
- Improve engraving precision for accurate image and PCB trace generation.
- Validate the performance of the newly fabricated machine through comprehensive testing.
